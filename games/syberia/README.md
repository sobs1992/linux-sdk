# Syberia

Syberia (2002, Microids; Virtools engine, Direct3D 7 through CKDX7Rasterizer,
Bink cinematics), the Russian edition 1.0, under Wine on the GO Ultra
(manifest `[syberia]`, wine.ini in `overlay/`).

- Runs as installed, no patch: the menus, subtitles and voices of the
  Russian edition; no CD check (the copy plays without a disc).
- 800x600 in 16-bit colour (the game's default Config.dat, made in My
  Documents on the first start), shown whole, 640x480 with bars at the
  sides: the scenes are the whole picture, nothing to crop.
- 30 fps steady (WINEBARE_FPS=1) in the scenes and the cinematics, the
  frame limit 30.
- Saved games: My Documents\Syberia Saves in the Wine prefix on /data.
- The left stick is the mouse, A and B its buttons, F1 Esc (the menu,
  skipping a cinematic).
